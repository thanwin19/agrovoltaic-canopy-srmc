# Documents and papers you still need (and where to get them)

The study is computer-only. These items turn assumptions into evidence.

| # | Document | Why it matters | Where to get it | Affects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rope datasheets (SS316 7x19 16 mm / 20 mm, 7x7 4 mm): breaking load, modulus, certificates | Sets the structural result; the guideline 250 kN WLL and spec 13 kN look inconsistent with typical values | Any wire-rope supplier / manufacturer data sheet; EN 12385-4 | R3 structure |
| 2 | PV module datasheet and IEC 61215/61730 certificates (efficiency, temperature coefficient, weight, size) | Replaces the generic 21 % module; sets kWp, weight and wind loads | Module supplier | R4, R3 |
| 3 | IS 875 (Part 3): 2015 and its wind-speed map | Confirm Vb = 39 m/s for Madurai, k-factors, pressure coefficients for open canopies | Bureau of Indian Standards / college library | R3 |
| 4 | Soil test report for the TCE ground (pH, EC, organic carbon, texture, infiltration) | Replaces the library soil (red sandy loam) | Soil testing laboratory (e.g. TNAU / district soil lab) | R5, R7 |
| 5 | Geotechnical report (bearing capacity, pull-out of ground anchors) | Anchors carry ~60-85 kN per stay in the checked design | Geotechnical consultant | R3 |
| 6 | Local weather and radiation data (IMD / AWS near Madurai) | Compare NASA/ERA5 data with ground data; hourly series for control | IMD data supply / TNAU weather station | R4, R6 |
| 7 | Shade-crop evidence for ginger, turmeric, coriander, amaranth (light response, yield, cost of cultivation) | Crop parameters are typical values; need local numbers | TNAU crop production guides, ICAR, journal papers (Marrou 2013, Dupraz 2011) | R5, R6, R7 |
| 8 | Crop price series (mandi) | Replace library prices | Agmarknet / local market | R7 |
| 9 | Tariff basis: PPA term sheet or open-access charges for an industrial buyer | Tariff (INR 3/kWh) and farmer share (30 %) are assumptions that drive the farmer income | TNERC orders, utility, a letter of intent from an industry | R7 |
| 10 | PM-KUSUM and state scheme guidelines | Subsidy sweep 0-30 % needs a real eligibility basis | MNRE / state nodal agency | R7 |
| 11 | Grid emission factor (CEA CO2 Baseline Database, latest) | Replace the approximate 0.71 kg/kWh | CEA website | R7 |
| 12 | Prior-art / patent search record (Indian Patent Office, Google Patents, Espacenet) | Any novelty claim needs it; this study does not provide one | Search with keywords: agrivoltaic, wire-driven tilt, rainwater harvesting panel roof, pyranometer tilt control | R7, patent |
| 13 | Permission or own photos for TCE street-view imagery | Street-view screenshots were used only as a visual reference in the renders; do not publish them | Take your own photographs of the Main Building and ground | R5 |
