## Before submitting

1. **Use the official template of the journal.** These files follow each publisher's general instructions only. I could not open every journal's author instructions, and the abstract limits I found online conflicted (IEEE Access 250 or 300 words, Energies and Solar Energy unconfirmed). The 193-word abstract is under all of those figures, but confirm on the journal's page.
2. Fill the **[placeholders]**: corresponding author, e-mail, ORCID, author contributions, funding, competing interests, suggested reviewers.
3. The cover letters contain a bracket for the authors to confirm that the work is original and not under review elsewhere. Do not remove it without confirming.
4. References are numbered; change to the journal's style if needed, and check each reference.
5. Run the similarity (plagiarism) check required by the college.
6. Every result is simulation. The sensors are software models. The month run has real weather but simulated sensors. The paper says so; keep those sentences.
7. Scope fit (my judgement): best fits are IEEE Access, Energies and Energy, Sustainability and Society; Solar Energy, Computers and Electronics in Agriculture and Agronomy will probably ask for measured field or crop data.
