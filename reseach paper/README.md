# reseach paper: manuscripts for the final-year project

Everything here is a **draft for the authors**; nothing has been submitted anywhere.

- `common/`: the **common (general) paper** `SRMC_Research_Paper` (no project or prototype section), its camera-ready sample, the project paper in IEEE layout, and the prior-art search.
- One folder per publisher, one folder per journal inside it, each with `SRMC_Manuscript_<journal>.docx/.pdf` and `Cover_letter_DRAFT_<journal>.docx/.pdf`:

| Publisher | Journal (chosen by me; check its aims and scope) | Template used |
|---|---|---|
| IEEE | IEEE Access | **Official IEEE Access Word template** (Access-Template-2024.docx, ieeeaccess.ieee.org), filled with its own styles |
| MDPI | Energies; Agronomy | **Official MDPI Word templates** of each journal (energies-template.dot, agronomy-template.dot, the links on each journal's Instructions for Authors page), filled with the MDPI_* styles |
| Taylor and Francis | International Journal of Green Energy | **Official T&F Word template** (TF_Template_Word_Windows_2016, from the T&F Author Services formatting page), filled with its styles |
| Springer Nature | Energy, Sustainability and Society | Springer Nature Word template (the copy I could download is on a conference website; confirm against the official download) |
| Elsevier | Solar Energy; Computers and Electronics in Agriculture | no required Word template (Elsevier accepts any tidy format at first submission): one column, Highlights, line numbers. The official LaTeX class is `elsarticle` (LaTeX is not installed here, so I could not build it) |

**The manuscripts are the project paper**: they are based on Reviews 0 to 5 (a table of the six reviews and their results), describe the **digital twin** (with a screenshot of the final twin, Figure 6), include the simulated October 2023 month driven by real NASA POWER weather, and the photo of the scaled prototype (Figure 7) (captioned as context only; no result comes from it). The abstract has 193 words.

## Before submitting

1. **Open the files in Microsoft Word.** The MDPI first page is a two-part layout that LibreOffice (used for the PDFs here) shows wrongly; export the PDF from Word for submission. I could not open every journal's author instructions, and the abstract limits I found online conflicted (IEEE Access 250 or 300 words, Energies and Solar Energy unconfirmed). The 193-word abstract is under all of those figures, but confirm on the journal's page.
2. Fill the **[placeholders]**: corresponding author, e-mail, ORCID, author contributions, funding, competing interests, suggested reviewers.
3. The cover letters contain a bracket for the authors to confirm that the work is original and not under review elsewhere. Do not remove it without confirming.
4. References are numbered; change to the journal's style if needed, and check each reference.
5. Run the similarity (plagiarism) check required by the college.
6. Every result is simulation. The sensors are software models. The month run has real weather but simulated sensors. The paper says so; keep those sentences.
7. Scope fit (my judgement): best fits are IEEE Access, Energies and Energy, Sustainability and Society; Solar Energy, Computers and Electronics in Agriculture and Agronomy will probably ask for measured field or crop data.

8. **IEEE Access** (from its official submission checklist): a Word or LaTeX file and a matching PDF are both required; every author needs a short biography and photograph below the references (placeholders are in the file); the submitting author needs a public ORCID; abbreviations must be defined at first use; no concurrent submission.
9. **MDPI:** the abstract is a single paragraph under 200 words with 6 keywords (template rule). MDPI wants its own reference style (Author, A.B.; ... Journal Year, Volume, pages); the references here are numbered but still in IEEE style, so convert them.
10. **Taylor and Francis (International Journal of Green Energy):** the only instructions PDF I could download (files.taylorandfrancis.com/ljgeauth.pdf, probably old) asks for an abstract of at most 100 words, 3 to 5 key words and Chicago-style references, so the T&F file has a shortened 100-word abstract and 5 keywords. The T&F site blocked my browser (a bot check I did not try to bypass), so check the current instructions yourself.
11. **Not available to me:** templates and instructions on sites that block automated access. Anything I could not read is marked as unverified above.
