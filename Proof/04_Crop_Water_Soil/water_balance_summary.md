# Rain harvest and irrigation (per hectare per year)

Rain 959 mm; catchment (sealed canopy, 99.9 % cover) 9988 m2; harvested 4650 m3 into a 100 m3 cistern; irrigation demand 4927 m3; supplied from the cistern 4625 m3 (94 %); borewell top-up 302 m3; overflow 2818 m3.

Method: daily FAO-56 style root-zone balance (Hargreaves-Samani ET0, crop coefficient Kc, depletion fraction p, yield response Ky); rain events of 5 mm or more close the canopy flat and fill the cistern after a 0.5 mm first flush at 85 % runoff.
