What you are looking at

Crops and solar panels both need the sun. This is a computer model of one farm canopy: solar panels on wires, 4.5 m above crops, that tilt by themselves so the plants get enough light and the panels still make electricity. In rain the panels lie flat and the water runs into a tank for irrigation. In a storm they stand edge-on so the wind passes through.

How far to trust it (please read)

  • It is a simulation study. No field data yet. The sensor values you see are simulated unless a real board is plugged in.
  • Checked against other tools: the solar output is within about 5 % of the PVGIS data service, the shadow model agrees with a 3-D ray tracer, and the web model and the MATLAB model give the same answers.
  • Assumed, not measured: electricity price, farmer share, costs, crop prices, soil and crop values.
  • Accuracy is measured against the controller's own goal (half electricity, half crop light), not against real crop yield in a field.
