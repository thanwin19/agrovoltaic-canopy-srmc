How it decides (every step): anemometer at or above 15 m/s (54 km/h) → storm stow, panels edge-on at 90° rain sensor wet → flat roof (3°) to the gutter; pyranometer below 20 W/m² → night park; otherwise it tries every tilt from 15° to 60° in 0.5° steps and picks the one with the best combined score = 50 % electricity + 50 % crop light value. The PAR sensor corrects the model (PAR ÷ pyranometer against what the model predicts). DHT11 (hot, dry air) and soil moisture (dry soil) lower the light the crop needs, so the panels accept more shade. Year-long test (864 cases, noisy sensors): 99.6 % of the best possible combined score, 82 % of the best electricity, 98 % of the best crop light value, tilt error 4.8°.
