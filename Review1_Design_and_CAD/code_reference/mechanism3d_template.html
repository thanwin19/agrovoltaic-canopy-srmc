Step by step: from sensor to panel

The lever arm, side view

Blue bar: the panel. White bar: the lever arm on the end of the tube, fixed at 45°. Dashed: the flat position. The wire moves the arm tip by d, which turns the tube.

What is real and what is drawn

Sizes follow the strength-checked final design: 0.44 m arm, 114 x 4 mm tube, 16 mm carrier rope, 6 mm drive wire, 120 mm drum, 10 kN winch. Wires and ropes are drawn about 4 times thicker so they can be seen. The animation is much faster than the real motor, which would take a few minutes for a full sweep. The scenario tilt angles are examples of what the controller chooses. This is a design model, not a photo of a built system, and anchors and foundations still need a site soil test and an engineer's check.
