How accurate is the model and the six-sensor controller?

Simple scorecard: what we checked, against what, and what we have not checked.

One-line answer for the judge: the sun, shadow and power calculations are checked against independent references and agree within about 5 %. Crop yield, water and money are model results with assumed inputs, so we do not give them an accuracy figure.

Three checks we ran (all with proof files)

1. Solar power vs PVGIS (European Commission tool)

95 %

Yearly energy is +5.0 % high (1,491 vs 1,419 kWh per kWp). The sunshine input itself differs by +5.2 %, so the power model adds almost no extra error.

Month by month the difference is between −1 % and +11 % (largest in the cloudy monsoon months Jun to Nov).

Proof: pv_validation.txt

2. Shadows vs Blender 3-D ray tracing

96 %

96 % of 80 test points are within 0.10 of the 3-D reference; the average difference is only 0.010 (1 % of lit area). Worst single case 0.22.

Proof: shading_validation.txt

3. Web twin vs MATLAB (two separate programs)

100 %

22 outputs agree to 0.0000 % (power, water, money, light, yields). This shows no coding error between the two versions. It does not prove the physics is right.

Proof: crosscheck_js_vs_matlab.txt

What we do not claim

  • We do not claim one overall accuracy percentage. Each part has its own check, or none.
  • PVGIS is itself a model, not a measurement. Our check shows agreement with a trusted tool, not with a power meter on our field.
  • No field or prototype measurements exist yet. The next step is to log real sensor and power data for one month and compare.
